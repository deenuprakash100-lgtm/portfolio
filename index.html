About

About Me

I'm a Data Research Analyst and Business Analyst currently working at Simpliaxis, with over two years of experience turning sales, lead and CRM data into reports leadership can actually act on. My work sits at the intersection of data analysis and business operations — I don't just build reports, I understand why the business needs them.

Day to day, I work extensively with Salesforce and LeadSquared to manage CRM data, audit leads and monitor follow-up activity. I build recurring sales and performance reports in Excel, Google Sheets and Power BI, track KPIs across teams, and calculate sales incentive payouts based on defined business rules.

I also care about data quality — validating, cleaning and auditing records before they reach a dashboard — and I've supported day-to-day business operations while coordinating with and guiding a small team. I enjoy the process of taking messy, raw business data and shaping it into something clear enough to drive a decision.

  • Data analysis & sales reporting
  • CRM management & lead auditing
  • Dashboard development & KPI reporting
  • Incentive calculation & data quality
